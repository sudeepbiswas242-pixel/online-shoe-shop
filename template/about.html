Our Story

ShoeStore is an online shoe shopping platform developed to make footwear shopping simple, convenient and user-friendly.

Customers can browse available shoes, search products, filter shoes by category, add products to their cart and place orders online.

The system also provides an administration panel where authorized administrators can manage shoes, stock, prices and customer orders.
